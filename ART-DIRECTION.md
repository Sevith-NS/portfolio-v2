# After Hours

The art direction for sevith.com. Every decision below traces to a numbered
answer from the intake, so it can be argued with rather than guessed at.

---

## The brief, in Sevith's words

> **Q1** — "WOW I wanna build websites like him."
> **Q19** — "I was always fascinated and thrilled with sleek modern UI websites which makes u go wow I wanna build something like this when i grow up."

Those are the same sentence twenty years apart. **The site's job is to do to a
visitor exactly what those sites did to Sevith.** Everything here serves that.

**The place** (Q3): *"a universe of all cool stuff I have built."*
**The hour** (Q4): *"Sunset or late night like New York."*
**The feeling to avoid** (Q2): *"what the fuck is this, another AI generated slop."*
**The exit line** (Q10): *"This guy is so creative."*

**Why "After Hours":** it's Sevith's own reference (Q39, The Weeknd), it is
literally warm light against deep dark, and it covers *"visibly in progress, I
keep on learning"* (Q8) — after hours is when you build your own things.

**The key light is in the answers.** Q45: *"a warm table lamp and my cat next to
it."* That lamp is the light source for the entire site. Everything is lit by it.

## Material: film, not paper

Settled against Sevith's first instinct, on the evidence of his own references:
a universe (Q3), late-night New York (Q4), Interstellar / The Odyssey / The Dark
Knight Rises (Q40), *After Hours* (Q39), Ghost of Tsushima / RDR2 / NFS Heat /
Avatar (Q44), 3D interactive Awwwards sites (Q46), an actual reel (Q98), weighty
Locomotive-style scroll (Q90).

Paper produces *"nice, tasteful."* It does not produce *"WOW I wanna build
websites like him."* **Dark is the real site. Light mode is the same world at
golden hour, on oat.**

## Colour

Oat and lapis survive (Q61) — Sevith proposed the warm pairing himself, and
named terracotta/rust and burgundy/plum as the high-contrast options.

| Role | Dark (the real mode) | Light (golden hour) |
|---|---|---|
| Base | cool near-black, never pure #000 | oat |
| Raised surface | one step up, still cool | oat sheet |
| Ink | warm off-white — oat at night | near-black, warm |
| **Lapis** — anything interactive | the existing light lapis | the existing deep lapis |
| **Rust / terracotta** — the lamp | warm, earthy, never neon | deeper, burnt |
| **Maroon** — depth, shadow, weight | | |

- One accent doing the work, a small palette behind it (Q62), **not loud** (Q63).
- **Refused: yellow, lime, any neon** (Q64). **No pastels** (Q65) — so the
  existing pastel project plates and frosted glass go; they are daylight language.
- **Grain on everything** (Q66). Gradients only in dark, only where earned (Q67).
- Shadows situational, not global (Q68).
- Lapis and maroon both carry personal meaning (Q70).

## Light

Warm, low, directional. Lamp and sunset — never overhead daylight. If a surface
is lit, something in the composition explains where the light came from.

## Type

Four voices. Sevith likes Coolvetica / Helvetica / Futura, not Caslon (Q47) —
clean, modern, geometric. He loves all-caps (Q67/Q49), wants mono as a mix of
everywhere and accents (Q50), sentence case for prose (Q56), huge type only
where it must hit (Q52), industry-standard tracking (Q53).

| Voice | Job |
|---|---|
| **Wide geometric caps** | Headlines, wordmark, section marks. This is the register Azonix and Stretch Pro live in (Q55) — Archivo's width axis reaches it, so no new dependency. |
| **A display serif, swapped** | Statements and outcomes only. Sevith likes the current pairing but says it can be better (Q58) — so the role survives, the face changes to something higher-contrast and more filmic. |
| **Geist** | Body and UI. Neutral, gets out of the way. |
| **Geist Mono** | Labels, numbers, metadata. Numbers are a feature (Q57). |

**Handwriting survives only where it is clearly intentional** (Q51) — two places
at most, down from every section. Sevith is "not a big fan".

## Structure

- **Separate pages, not one long scroll** (Q71) — *"who reads everything in one
  page."* If a page does run long, it earns it with scroll choreography.
- **Order: what I offer + my name → who I am → the work → wrap** (Q72, Q73).
- **Nav: yes, absolutely** (Q74).
- **Off-grid, get creative** (Q75). **Spacious, real white space** (Q76).
  **Centred** (Q77).
- **Mobile first, tested on phone first** (Q12) — his Google UX takeaway. Laptop
  is where most will look, but the phone is where it gets designed.
- **5–10 seconds** to land (Q16). The offer has to read before anything moves.
- **Case studies: long-form, own page each**, with device mockups (Q97) and
  walkthroughs (Q79).
- **Studio: side page** (Q78), and it needs better models — the current one is
  the thing Sevith would delete today (Q112).
- **Type specimen survives** (Q80). **Resume is a PDF link, no page** (Q81).
- **Impossible to miss** (Q82): how he builds, what he offers, and that he is
  creative.

## Motion

- **Not a lot. Never distracting** (Q83).
- **Scroll-driven** (Q84), with the **weighty Lenis feel kept** — he likes the
  scroll and dislikes plain scroll (Q90, Q91, Q92).
- **Keep the light/dark transition** — slow and weighty. He'd fight for it (Q111).
- **Hover effects: loved** (Q86). Keep and extend.
- **Cursor: keep the current one**, expanded on case studies (Q87).
- **Kill the marquee** (Q88) — *"looks completely diff from the page style."*
- **Section transitions** in the spirit of gravity-design.de (Q35).
- **An easter egg, yes please** (Q89). The mechanic is the cats: **Simba**
  explores and always finds his way home; **Cream** makes chaos (Q33, Q34).

## Voice

First person (Q101), **short and punchy** (Q103), **demonstrate the claim rather
than announce it** (Q104). Swearing is allowed (Q102) — placed, not sprayed.

**Hero line:** *"I design it, then I actually build it."*

Teach the role rather than assume it (Q13) — the register to match is
rachelchen.tech's *"product designer who engineers."*

**Digital.ai is downplayed** (Q106). The lead is clean UI and end-to-end product
engineering for **startups, businesses and freelance clients** (Q11, Q18). The
enterprise GenAI launch becomes a supporting credential, not the pitch.

**Jokes land after a scroll, not on the first screen** (Q7).

**His line, to use somewhere:** *"Not everyone has great taste."* (Q107)

**Personality material, for the asides** (Q20–Q34): a self-taught designer who
ships what he just learned; a full-time online meme collector; *"gotta stack up
the Gandhis"*; peri peri fried chicken with mac and cheese; overspends on fits;
wanted to be *"rich af"* at 14; openly hates DSA; four cats named **Simba, Ash,
Pearl and Cream**; watches a film or irritates a cat when stuck.

**Never:** *"I don't understand"* (Q108). And nothing that reads as AI slop (Q2) —
no "elevate", no "seamless", no "passionate about leveraging".

## Audience

**Primary** (Q11, Q14, Q18): people hiring talent — freelance clients, startups
and businesses who want clean UI plus end-to-end product engineering. Employers
and clients both. A non-technical recruiter may be in the chain (Q17), so the
offer must survive someone who doesn't know the job title.

**Judged by** (Q115): Reddit, and Sevith's own taste.

## Steal / avoid

**Steal** (Q35–Q36):
- **rachelchen.tech** — case-study format, video woven in, and the plain-language
  role line.
- **pamidordesign.co** — the loading screen, the font pairing, the structure.
- **lil-boxes.com** — typography, and sections that stack with fun elements.
- **gravity-design.de** — the section transitions.
- **columbus-travel.com** — the model if a page does go long-scroll (Q71).

**Avoid** (Q37, Q38): cluttered scroll-timeline sites; product.inc, which he
read as *AI-generated and cluttered* — that is the failure mode to design
against. Modern-Netflix flatness (Q41).

**Voice references** (Q42): Zomato, chriswillx.
**Visual references** (Q43): Aevy, Zero1 by Zerodha, Aperture, Hyperplexed.

## Constraints

- **Applying now** (Q109). The current site stays sendable on `save-with-case`
  while this is built on `after-hours`.
- **Full rebuild** (Q110).
- **Keep:** the light/dark transition, the scroll weight (Q111).
- **Dependencies: open** (Q114), with a `cloudflare/security-audit-skill` pass
  and a frontend-skill critique before it ships.
- All copy lives in `data/` so Sevith can edit it without touching components
  (Q113).
- Photos: plenty available, posed-candid, outdoors (Q93, Q94). **Cats yes**
  (Q95). **No desk** (Q96). Face on `/story` only, not home (Q100).
